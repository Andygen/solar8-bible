# 4-5 — Assembler Prime / Мегасборщик

Bible reviewed 2026-09-29: scenario.html#mission-4-5, fleet.html and
resource-status.html. Published shared dialogue records are unchanged. The master
still omits 44 local additions from 4-2 to 4-4; import correctly rejected their
deletion. This mission adds 15 canonical lines with Russian drafts and story IDs,
plus three clearly marked bilingual briefing adaptations.

## Implemented battle

- Phase 1: four animated manipulators (16 HP each), production of pairs of Mars
  Scouts with visible bay warnings, and telegraphed cutting lanes. Destroying a
  tool stops its movement and cancels its pending cutting attack. Drones spawn
  from the body and are capped at four active at wave start.
- Phase 2: two exposed repair modules (12 HP each), 60 HP central armor, five-shot
  aimed fans. Each surviving module restores three armor HP every two seconds.
  The armor cannot trigger phase three while a repair module survives. Disabling
  both modules permanently stops regeneration.
- Phase 3: bay opens, press retracts, foreign core appears (42 HP), seven-shot fans.
  During the canonical phase dialogue, Max's five-second scan runs through
  ROOK's remote sensor link. Scan starts at phase second 11, after Andygen's line.
  The core is protected while scanning so automatic fire cannot skip the story
  objective. HUD shows preparation, percentage and vulnerability. Destroy the core
  after the scan and clear any remaining drones to finish.

Ending: clean SOLAR fragment, interrupted channel, ROOK traces the relay vector to
Jupiter. Existing framed portraits are reused; SOLAR uses the dedicated unknown
channel screen. The optional proposed Mars→Jupiter hangar upgrade is not enabled:
its equipment effect belongs with the unimplemented Jupiter campaign.

Balance values are implementation proposals, not numerical Bible canon. Cutting
lanes warn for 1.5 seconds, stay active 0.35 seconds and hit only within the drawn
footprint, once per strike. Phase-two fans recur every 3.2 seconds at 210 px/s;
phase-three fans recur every 2.8 seconds at 230 px/s. They aim at the player's
current position and do not home. Stars: scan/destroy the core, prevent all armor
regeneration, take no hull damage. Boss score is awarded once.

Available after 4-4 and from the Mars selector. After 4-5 the report returns to the
menu; no unfinished Jupiter mission is launched. No APK exported.

port preserves the source pivots, layer order and Canvas pose formulas. Fingers,
saw, drill arm, hatches and press move independently. Collision targets follow the
animated wrists. Damaged arms darken and stop; these are not pre-rendered phase
swaps. The original phase PNGs remain untouched. See the runtime rig README.

All ten runtime PNGs are below 500 KB with alpha preserved. New assembly-ring.png
background is 784278 bytes. Its master is outside exports in build/bible-4-5 and
in the Bible handoff. Background generated with built-in imagegen from the current
scenario; it is a proposed asset, not previously approved art. Prompt in handoff.

## Validation

assembler_smoke tests tool movement and physical bullet collision, attack
cancellation, cutting-lane bounds, armor repair from two/one/zero modules,
non-bypassable repair phase, protected core, five-second scan, actual core
collision after unlocking, single score award, star conditions and all five
ending lines. Russian screenshots cover each phase and SOLAR's channel.

assembler_playthrough uses real single-stream 1-damage bullets at 0.32 s intervals,
prioritizes immediate drones/tools/modules, and runs all three phases to victory.
The automated pilot is invulnerable: this validates completion, not human
survivability or final difficulty. Runtime and localization checks also cover 4-4.
Human playtesting is the next step, with changes reviewed one level at a time.
