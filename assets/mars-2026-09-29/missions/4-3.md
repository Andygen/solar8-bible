## Canon and gameplay

ROOK reads NOVA through an isolated service interface with no route to flight
control. The player protects physical memory nodes. Max saves only a small,
isolated clean personality fragment; NOVA is not fully cured. All 13 story lines
and Russian drafts are copied from Bible, with story IDs retained. Four briefing
lines explain gameplay and are marked adaptations. All additions have stable
dialogue IDs and English/Russian text.

Implementation balance, proposed for Bible: three nodes with 10 HP each, a
24-second introduction, 60 seconds of active reading, four-second interruptions
every 22 seconds, nine pairs of attackers at eight-second intervals. Attackers
approach nodes and telegraph aimed shots with red beams. Player bullets cannot
damage friendly nodes. Losing any node fails the mission. Completion requires
ROOK to return and all surviving attackers to be cleared before the fragment is
saved. Stars: save the fragment, take no node damage, take no hull damage.

Available from the Mars selector and after 4-2. The ending continues to the
implemented 4-4 Foreign Template mission.
