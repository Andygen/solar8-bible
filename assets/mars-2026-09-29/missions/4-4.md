## Canon

ROOK scans three assembling devices inside an undocumented white/black production
module within rusty Martian industry. Enemies attack samples and the pilot.
Required samples are immune to friendly fire; losing any one, including an
already-scanned device, immediately fails. Max obtains the first complete block
of foreign code, compatible with SOLAR but not written by a human system.
Vale restricts access. No later boss events are advanced.

Eleven canonical lines and Russian drafts are copied from Bible with story IDs;
three bilingual briefing adaptations explain the mechanics. Story lines after
scan three play in the six-line ending, followed by Vale's exchange. The first
scan triggers the Max/Lena geometry discussion. Existing framed portraits are
reused; Vale was visually compared with his current website reference.

## Gameplay implementation (balance proposal)

Three static devices, 10 HP each; 12-second discovery introduction, then 22
seconds of active scanning per device. ROOK physically moves A → B → C at 180
pixels/second and cannot read until in range. Devices show labels, health and
green completed-scan rings. All three remain vulnerable through extraction.

Every ten seconds a wave spawns: one attacker before the first scan, two after
it, three after the second. Final-stage waves include a Mars Striker. Reused Mars
Scout/Striker hulls approach samples and telegraph their sample shot with a red
beam; sample shots recur every 3.8 s. In this mission they also aim a 220 px/s
projectile at the player's current position every 4.8 s after entering the field.
These projectiles do not home. No new waves after the third scan; victory waits
for ROOK to dock and remaining enemies to be cleared.

Stars: scan and preserve all three samples; no sample damage; no hull damage.
Mission appears in the Mars selector and follows 4-3; 4-4 now continues to the
implemented 4-5 Assembler Prime mission. No APK exported.
