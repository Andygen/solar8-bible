Reviewed Bible 2026-09-28: scenario.html, scripts-earth-mars.html, fleet.html,

The published 282-line dialogue catalog imported without text differences.

## Canon and implementation

Assembly Cradles manufacture enemies on screen; Repair Units restore recently
destroyed heavy craft. The ending reveals unfamiliar components to Max and Lena.
All nine canonical scene lines and their Russian drafts are copied from the
current scenario page, with `story_id` retained in the handoff. Four bilingual
briefing lines explain the implemented mechanics and are marked adaptations.
No NOVA reveal or later-chapter story event was added.

Gameplay settings (not claimed as numerical Bible canon): three cradles, 24 HP
each, ten-second assembly cycles, at most seven active defenders; two initially
deployed Mars Guards. Repair Units enter at 12 and 42 seconds while production
remains active. A heavy wreck lasts 12 seconds, repair takes six seconds after
arrival, and each heavy unit can revive once. Killing the Repair Unit interrupts
the repair. A revival cannot award duplicate kill score. Destroyed cradles stop
producing immediately; victory requires shutting down all three and clearing
active defenders. Player shots pass through inactive wrecks.

Three stars: clear the factory, prevent all revivals, lose no hull HP.
These extra-star conditions are implementation proposals for Bible review.
The mission is accessible through the Mars map/service selector and after 4-1;
4-2 now continues to the implemented 4-3 NOVA mission.
