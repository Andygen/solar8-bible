# Mars 4-1 — Red Line

Canon checked 2026-09-22 against the live Bible:
- https://andygen.github.io/solar8-bible/index.html#mars
- https://andygen.github.io/solar8-bible/scripts-earth-mars.html
- https://andygen.github.io/solar8-bible/fleet.html#mars

Cross the industrial transport belt: four mag-rail cargo platforms and four
rotating industrial arms, nine waves of Mars Scout / Striker / Guard. Machines
warn for three seconds, move for six seconds and leave a navigable route.
Arm warnings identify the correct side, including the mirrored left-side event.
Counts, HP, timings and collision dimensions are gameplay tuning, not Bible facts.
The belt finishes only after all eight machines have passed, the wave queue is
finished and the final enemy has been cleared. No artificial victory explosion.

Stars require mission success: cross the belt; never contact machinery; lose no
hull HP to machinery. Shield absorption or invulnerability does not erase physical
contact, but does preserve the hull bonus. Enemy damage is not machinery damage.
Each crossing can register only one collision. Movement uses substeps on slow
frames. Orange warning paths have no collision damage; magnetic rails are guides.

ROOK remains the physical escort and announces machinery. Max / Andygen entry and
ending lines are from the Bible. The instructional briefing is adaptation, with
no new story facts. Live enemy assembly and repair belong to 4-2 and are absent.
The approved player Mars upgrade is selected automatically without new stat bonuses.
Earth 3-5 leads through the campaign map to Mars 4-1. As of 2026-09-29,
4-1 continues to 4-2, and the full Mars sequence 4-1 through 4-5 is implemented.

Assets and exact generation prompts: assets/art/mars/README.md. New machine PNGs
are concepts for review, not assets already approved by the website owner.
The website's existing Mars vista is only 384x512; it is reused unchanged.

Validation: red_line_smoke checks warnings, both arm sides, collision / shield /
immunity / hull accounting, correct fleet textures, all eight scheduled hazards,
nine waves, independent stars and canonical ending. Campaign and Citadel tests
cover the Earth-to-Mars transition. Visual captures cover loader and arm states.
Android export is explicitly requested for testing after this mission.

Historical 4-1 test build (not a build of the complete Mars chapter):
build/solar8-0.7.0-test.apk, package com.andygen.solar8,
versionCode 38, arm64-v8a, Android 7+ (min SDK 24). The signing certificate matches
0.6.3 and APK Signature Schemes v2/v3 verify. The APK contains the compiled Mars
scene, mission and machinery scripts and the imported new art. Local mission,
campaign, Citadel, briefing, ROOK, player-variant and HUD smoke suites passed.
Visual review covered 540x960 and 540x1170 windows. No physical-device playtest
was performed; that is the purpose of this delivery.

Reproduce with tools/export_android_local.ps1 and -Engine pointing to Godot 4.7.2.
It uses build/tools dependencies and portable settings, preserving installed Godot
settings and the previous app signing key. The isolated Windows root-certificate
lookup can fail under the sandbox; the exporter allows only that unrelated
startup message and independently verifies the signed APK.
