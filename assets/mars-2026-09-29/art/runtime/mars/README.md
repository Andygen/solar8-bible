## props/mag-rail-loader-concept.png

Create one production-ready PNG game sprite of a massive Mars Industrial mag-rail cargo loader platform for the vertical space shooter SOLAR 8. Strict orthographic TOP DOWN view, camera looking straight onto upper surface, no isometric tilt, no perspective. Wide horizontal rectangular heavy flatbed platform, approximately 3:1 silhouette, carrying three secured steel freight containers. Worn burnt-orange paint, dark graphite metal, piston mounts, realistic hard-surface detail, yellow-black safety stripes and small amber running lights. No cockpit, wings, weapons or jet exhaust: this is moving infrastructure on magnetic rails, not a spaceship. Platform isolated, entire silhouette visible, centered with tight but safe transparent margins, genuinely transparent background with alpha. No ground, rails, environment, cast shadow beyond object, text, UI, labels or checkerboard. Crisp realistic rendered bitmap compatible with the industrial orange fleet. Asset will travel horizontally across a combat corridor. Canvas landscape.

## props/industrial-arm-concept.png

Create one production-ready transparent PNG game sprite: a single enormous robotic industrial manipulator arm for Mars Industrial Network in the space shooter SOLAR 8. STRICT ORTHOGRAPHIC TOP DOWN, looking straight down onto upper surface. Arm lies horizontally: round fixed mounting pivot at far LEFT, long nearly straight horizontal articulated orange-and-graphite metal boom with two joint housings and exposed hydraulic pistons, open two-finger cargo gripper at far RIGHT. Overall long thin silhouette about 4:1. Pivot center at approximately 10 percent canvas width, centerline at 50 percent height. Entire object visible with small transparent margins. Worn burnt-orange steel, dark graphite metal, realistic industrial machining and bolts, restrained amber running lights, yellow-black safety stripes. No spacecraft, thrusters, characters, ground, surroundings, rails, text, labels, UI or checkerboard. Genuinely transparent alpha background. Realistic crisp rendered bitmap; arm will rotate as one silhouette around left mounting joint in a top-down mobile shooter. Landscape canvas.
