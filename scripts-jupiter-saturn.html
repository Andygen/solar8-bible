Jupiter + Saturn
full VO pass

Jupiter даёт первый точный внешний вектор и меняет отношения Andygen/Rex. Saturn раскрывает память SOLAR и тайну Admiral Vale.

В этих главах меньше шуток. По мере удаления от Земли диалоги становятся короче, а паузы важнее. Jupiter давит масштабом, Saturn — смыслом.

Chapter 5 — Jupiter

Штормовая среда, Captain Navarro, спасение Andygen Rex'ом и доказательство внешнего источника сигнала.

5-1

Storm Edge / «Граница шторма»

Первые wind-shear зоны и знакомство с Navarro.

Entry
NAVARRO
Storm Edge Control to Andygen. I have you on scope. Don't fight the wind. Cross it when it turns.
ANDYGEN
You make that sound easy.
NAVARRO
After twenty-six years, it gets less surprising.
First drift
ROOK P1
Wind shear shifting left. Compensate now.
ANDYGEN
You could've mentioned the planet moves the ship for me.
NAVARRO
Jupiter is helpful like that.
Signal anomaly
ROOK
Command pulse detected. Direction inconsistent with local SOLAR traffic.
MAYA
Save the vector. I'll compare it with Venus noise.
Mission end
NAVARRO
If you're chasing signals in this atmosphere, you're either very good or very stupid.
ANDYGEN
I get that a lot.
ROOK
Both classifications remain plausible.
5-2

Lightning Wall / «Молниевая стена»

Электрические фронты и кратковременные потери сенсоров.

Approach
NAVARRO
Lightning front ahead. Don't trust the bright ones.
ANDYGEN
Which ones do I trust?
NAVARRO
The quiet gaps.
Storm sectors
ROOK P1
Electrical saturation rising. Sector three unsafe.
ROOK P1
Discharge imminent.
EMP event
ROOK
Sensor suite degraded. Restoring tactical overlay.
ANDYGEN
I can still see the big planet.
ROOK
Encouraging.
Mission end
NAVARRO
You made it through the wall. Next one's worse.
ANDYGEN
You really know how to welcome guests.
5-3

Red Spot / «Красное пятно»

Вихри, потеря управления и первый серьёзный поступок Rex.

ROOK

Rex save

REX

ROOK
For the record, he did not.
Post-mission
ANDYGEN
I owe you one.
REX
Don't make me collect.
5-4

Vector / «Вектор»

Три точки триангуляции и первый точный внешний курс.

Scan point one
MAYA
First baseline locked. Hold position.
ROOK P2
EMP wave incoming. Scan integrity at risk.

LENA

MAX

MAYA

ROOK

ANDYGEN
How far outside?
ROOK
Beyond Saturn. Direction remains unresolved.
VALE
Nobody shares that result outside this channel.
5-5

Eye of the Storm / «Око бури»

Tempest Crown, внешний передатчик и путь к Saturn archive.

Boss reveal

That's the old storm relay. Or what's left of it.
ROOK
Power output exceeds original design by four hundred percent.

ROOK P2

Get there first. Then we talk.

Chapter 6 — Saturn

Красивый high-speed вход сменяется тихим архивом и самым личным конфликтом первой половины истории.

6-1

Rings / «Кольца»

Скорость, лёд и визуальная передышка перед тяжёлым сюжетом.

Entry
MAYA
I could fly this all day.
ANDYGEN
Finally, a planet with taste.
ROOK
Saturn is not responsible for your aesthetic approval.
Dense ring lane
ROOK P1
Large ice body crossing. Three seconds.
MAYA
Take the gap under it.
Archive detected
LENA
Mnemosyne Archive is still powered. Barely.
MAX
Barely is my favorite condition. Means nobody updated it.
6-2

Ice Stream / «Ледяной поток»

Используем debris как укрытие и оружие.

Combat rule
ROOK
Large fragments can block incoming fire.
ANDYGEN
And outgoing?
ROOK
Physics remains impartial.
Collision opportunity
MAYA
Push that heavy into the ice lane.
ANDYGEN
Now you're speaking my language.
Lena arrival
LENA
I'm linked to Mnemosyne. Keep the archive isolated until I say otherwise.
MAX
Welcome back.
LENA
Your sandbox still explodes?
MAX
Only when it's working.

6-4

Archive playback

RULE
Не оправдывается многословно. Чем сильнее Andygen злится, тем короче и спокойнее отвечает Vale.
