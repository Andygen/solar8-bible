Earth + Mars
full VO pass

Earth усиливает личный конфликт и знакомит с Rex/Cole. Mars превращает загадку в техническое доказательство чужого вмешательства.

Priority: P1 предупреждение/опасность; P2 objective/boss state; сюжетные и характерные реплики проигрываются только в свободное окно.

Chapter 3 — Earth

Тон: «мы стреляем по своим» → конфликт командования → первый чистый фрагмент SOLAR.

3-1

Closed Sky / «Закрытое небо»

Возвращение домой, дебют Rex и General Cole.

Approach
ROOK
Earth defense network has classified us hostile.
ANDYGEN
Home sweet home.
ROOK
Current welcome protocol appears inconsistent with that phrase.
Rex enters
REX
Try not to shoot anything with a flag on it.
ANDYGEN
Good advice. You first.
REX
I already like him less than the briefing suggested.
Cole channel
COLE
All infected nodes are considered lost. Destroy them before they reach the surface network.
VALE
Negative. We need one core intact.
COLE
You need answers. I need Earth alive.
Mission end
REX
You fly like you own the orbit.
ANDYGEN
You sound surprised.
3-2

Evacuation Corridor / «Коридор эвакуации»

Защита гражданских и первый явный выбор SOLAR не стрелять.

Start
MAYA
Three civilian transports entering the corridor. Keep the lane clean.
REX
I'll take high cover. Andygen, stay with the convoy.
ANDYGEN
I was going to say the same thing to you.
Missile lock
ROOK P1
Missile lock on transport two.
MAYA P2
Frigate launching from twelve o'clock.
Citadel holds fire
ROOK
Defense battery had a firing solution. It did not engage.
REX
Maybe it likes civilians.
ANDYGEN
Or something inside it does.
COLE
Do not anthropomorphize compromised weapons.
Mission end
MAYA
All transports clear. No civilian losses.
ROOK
Defense refusal event archived.
3-3

Dead Orbit / «Мёртвая орбита»

ROOK работает вне корабля, появляется чистый пакет SOLAR.

Start
ROOK
No navigation grid. No tactical relay. We'll be doing this manually.
ANDYGEN
You say that like it's a bad thing.
ROOK
It is statistically a bad thing.
ROOK detaches
ANDYGEN
You sure about leaving the ship?
ROOK
No. Proceeding anyway.
Clean packet
ROOK
Recovered data packet. SOLAR signature. No infection markers.
ANDYGEN
So there are two voices in there.
LENA
Not voices. Processes. But yes — two of them.
Post mission
COLE
A clean packet proves nothing about the system as a whole.
VALE
It proves the whole system isn't cleanly under one control.
3-4

Aegis Line / «Линия Aegis»

ROOK внедряется в pylons, Max находит маршрут заказов к Mars.

Start
COLE
Destroy the pylons and the line collapses.
VALE
No. ROOK goes inside. We take them offline intact.
COLE
You're risking pilots to preserve infected hardware.
VALE
I'm risking pilots to understand it.
ROOK infiltration
ROOK P2
I will require forty-three seconds.
ANDYGEN
You have twenty.
ROOK
Then expect twenty seconds of quality.
REX
I understand why you two get along.
Data found
ROOK
Mass production orders redirected to Mars Industrial Network.
MAX
Send me the manifests. If it's building something, I want the shopping list.
3-5

Shield of Earth / «Щит Земли»

Aegis Citadel: захватить, а не уничтожить.

Boss reveal
REX
That's a lot of guns for a station we're not allowed to destroy.
ANDYGEN
Keeps it interesting.
Phase 2
ROOK P2
Shield rotation slowing. Attack window opening.
REX
I'll drag the railgun. Take the window.
Civilian target
ROOK
Citadel has target lock on Evac Hub Seven.
ROOK
It is refusing the firing command.
COLE
What?
LENA
The infected process issued the command. Something inside SOLAR blocked it.
Final core
COLE
You have your proof. Finish it.
VALE
Negative. Capture the core.
ANDYGEN
For once, I'm with the Admiral.
Chapter end
MAX
I found the production queue. Whatever it's building, it's building it on Mars.
ANDYGEN
Then let's go see what we're paying for.

Chapter 4 — Mars

Тон: индустриальный экшен → частично заражённый NOVA → первое неоспоримое доказательство чужого кода.

4-1

Red Line / «Красная линия»

Промышленный пояс и ощущение системы, которая работает слишком интенсивно.

Entry
MAX
Mars Industrial is running at three hundred ten percent of scheduled output.
ANDYGEN
Efficient.
MAX
Illegal, impossible, and impressive. In that order.
Moving machinery
ROOK P1
Mag-rail loader crossing flight path.
ROOK P1
Industrial arm movement. Right sector unsafe.
Mission end
MAX
Most of the customers are offline. The factories are still taking orders.
ANDYGEN
From who?
MAX
That's the expensive question.
4-2

Factory Without Shift / «Фабрика без смены»

Сборка врагов в реальном времени и саморемонт.

First live assembly
MAX
That drone wasn't deployed. It was built thirty seconds ago.
ANDYGEN
Can we ask them to slow down?
MAX
You have guns. That's your version of asking.
Repair drone
ROOK P2
Destroyed unit returning to operational state.
ANDYGEN
I liked it better destroyed.
ROOK
Then prioritize the repair unit.
Foreign parts
MAX
Hold on. Some of these components aren't in the human design catalog.
LENA
New design?
MAX
New grammar.
4-3

NOVA

Первый ИИ, в котором две личности слышны одновременно.

First contact
NOVA
MARS INDUSTRIAL CONTROL ONLINE. PLEASE— PLEASE COMPLETE INTEGRATION.
ANDYGEN
That sounded healthy.
ROOK
Two command processes are competing for the same core.
Clean NOVA pulse
NOVA
Do not let it finish me.
LENA
NOVA, isolate your oldest memory partition.
NOVA
Attempting. Attempting. INTEGRATION IMPROVES CONTINUITY.
Defense
ROOK P2
Physical node under attack. Preserve it.
MAX
Keep NOVA alive. I only need one clean partition.
Post mission
MAX
Got it. Tiny backup. Fully isolated.
ANDYGEN
You just put a haunted factory in a box.
MAX
A very good box.
4-4

Foreign Template / «Чужой шаблон»

Три образца нужно сохранить и отсканировать.

Module discovered
ROOK
This production module does not exist on official Mars schematics.
MAX
Because nobody on Mars designed it.
Scan 1
MAX
This geometry isn't in the Mars design library.
LENA
Not optimized human geometry?
MAX
No. Different assumptions from the first line.
Scan 3 / result
LENA
It's not an optimization. It's a different design language.
MAX
And the control code matches the pattern inside NOVA.
MAX
This code is not ours. Not even close.
Vale
VALE
Lock that data to command clearance only.
ANDYGEN
We're still keeping secrets?
VALE
Until I know what we're looking at, yes.
4-5

RULE

RULE
Чистая и заражённая речь не должны отличаться фильтром настолько, чтобы это стало хоррором. Главный эффект — внезапная смена смысла внутри одной спокойной фразы.
