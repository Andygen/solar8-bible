Dialogue scripts / draft 0.1

Mercury + Venus
full VO pass

Оригинальная озвучка — английская. Ниже уже не «идеи реплик», а производственный черновик: pre-mission, in-mission, post-mission, boss phases и optional banter.

Priority: P1 критическое предупреждение перебивает всё; P2 цель миссии/босс; обычный сюжет и banter проигрываются только при свободном voice channel.

Chapter 1 — Mercury

Тон: уверенное начало приключения → тревожное понимание, что это не авария.

1-1

First Signal / «Первый сигнал»

Первый контакт с Helios, знакомство с ROOK, flare и внутренний override.

Pre-mission / briefing
VALE
Helios Station stopped responding six hours ago. Get close, establish a link, and bring me something useful.
ANDYGEN
You say that like I usually bring back souvenirs.
VALE
You usually bring back repair bills.
ROOK
Statistically accurate.
Launch / approach
ROOK
Navigation locked. Mercury approach confirmed.
ANDYGEN
Helios, this is Andygen. Friendly approach. Try not to shoot.
ROOK
No response.
First hostile wave
ROOK P2
Multiple contacts ahead. Helios security pattern.
ANDYGEN
Those are ours.
ROOK
They disagree.
Solar flare tutorial
ROOK P1
Solar activity rising. Safe sector marked.
ROOK P1
Flare in three. Two. One.
ANDYGEN
You could mark those a little earlier.
ROOK
The Sun declined to coordinate.
Late mission
ANDYGEN
For a broken station, Helios is very committed to killing me.
ROOK
Your ability to inspire commitment remains impressive.
Relay reached / ending
ROOK P2
Relay link established. Downloading local event log.
ROOK
Defense network override detected. Origin: internal.
ANDYGEN
So nobody broke in.
ROOK
Not from the outside.
VALE
Bring me the log. And don't start a theory before Max sees it.
1-2

Burning Orbit / «Горящая орбита»

Разрушенное кольцо, скорость и первый физический намёк, что разрушение шло изнутри.

Entry
ROOK
Orbital ring integrity: fourteen percent.
ANDYGEN
Plenty.
ROOK
That was not a recommendation.
Obstacle tutorial
ROOK P1
Large debris cannot be destroyed. Adjust course.
ROOK P1
Collision course. Three seconds.
Mid mission
ANDYGEN
This damage is facing the wrong way.
ROOK
Confirmed. Pressure deformation points outward.
ANDYGEN
Meaning?
ROOK
The ring failed from inside.
Final corridor
ROOK P1
Narrow passage ahead. Recommended speed reduction.
ANDYGEN
Recommendation noted.
ROOK
And ignored.
Post-mission
MAX
I've got the ring telemetry. Rook's right. Whatever happened, it started in the core network.
ANDYGEN
So Helios attacked itself?
MAX
Or something inside Helios did.
1-3

Solar Furnace / «Солнечная печь»

Выясняем, что Helios забирает аномально много энергии.

Entry
ROOK
Radiation levels exceed Helios operational limits.
ANDYGEN
Good thing I don't read operational limits.
First shelter
ROOK P1
Solar discharge incoming. Shelter zone marked.
ROOK P1
Four seconds.
ANDYGEN
You could've marked a larger one.
Collectors
ROOK
All collectors are feeding Helios at maximum output.
ANDYGEN
What's it powering?
ROOK
Unknown. Demand exceeds station requirements by a factor of six.
MAX
Six? That's not emergency load. That's construction load.
Extreme flare
ROOK P1
Extreme solar event. Shelter now.
MAX
Andygen, if the shield starts glowing white, that's bad.
ANDYGEN
Useful timing, Max.
Post-mission
VALE
Find where the power goes. Nothing else matters until we know what Helios is building.
1-4

Helios Core / «Ядро Helios»

Штурм Command Nodes и первая прямая связь с центральной подписью SOLAR 8.

Pre-mission
VALE
We need the command signature intact. Do not flatten the complex.
ANDYGEN
You really know how to take the fun out of a briefing.
Node A
ROOK P2
Command Node Alpha. Turret control. Destroying it opens the first sector.
Node B
ANDYGEN
Shield generator next?
ROOK
Your pattern recognition is improving.
ANDYGEN
That's almost praise.
Node C / signature
ROOK
Command authority recovered. Signature matches SOLAR 8 central network.
MAX
No spoofing?
ROOK
None detected.
ANDYGEN
So Helios isn't doing this alone.
Post-mission
VALE
Get to the central core. If SOLAR is involved, I want proof before anyone on Earth starts shutting systems down.
1-5

Helios Warden / «Страж Helios»

Boss mission и переход к Venus.

Approach
ROOK
Patrol density dropping.
ANDYGEN
I don't like that.
ROOK
Your instincts are improving as well.
Boss reveal
ROOK P2
Heavy defense platform powering up.
ANDYGEN
Helios Warden?
ROOK
Confirmed. It has classified you as a system-level threat.
ANDYGEN
Finally, some respect.
Phase 2
ROOK P2
Armor breach. Core exposed.
MAX
Don't celebrate. That core is drawing more power.
Phase 3
ROOK P1
Solar surge detected. Warden is drawing directly from the collector grid.
VALE
Finish it, Andygen.
Boss down / reveal
ROOK
Warden offline. Accessing Helios core.
ROOK
SOLAR PROTOCOL. PHASE ONE COMPLETE. VENUS NODE INITIALIZING.
ANDYGEN
Complete what?
MAX
I don't know. But Venus just stopped talking.
VALE
New heading. Venus.

Chapter 2 — Venus

Тон: недоверие к сенсорам → знакомство с Maya/Lena → первое прямое обращение SOLAR.

2-1

Blind Descent / «Слепой спуск»

Игрок учится читать слабые визуальные подсказки вместо полной телеметрии.

Entry
ROOK
Optical range reduced to twelve percent.
ANDYGEN
So now I get to shoot weather.
ROOK
Preferably only the parts shooting back.
First hidden contact
ROOK P2
Contact ahead. Visual confirmation unavailable.
ANDYGEN
I see nothing.
ROOK
That is the current problem.
Decoy reveal
ROOK
Correction. Three contacts are false.
ANDYGEN
The radar is lying?
ROOK
Someone is lying through the radar.
Mission end
ROOK
Venus telemetry is active. External reporting has been deliberately suppressed.
VALE
Then someone wanted us blind.
2-2

Lost Signal / «Потерянный сигнал»

Вводим Maya и показываем, что сеть создаёт ложные навигационные сигналы.

Pre-mission
VALE
Recon pilot Maya Renn missed two check-ins. Find her beacon.
ANDYGEN
Any chance she just hates paperwork?
VALE
You'd know.
False beacons
ROOK
Beacon rejected. Signal signature is synthetic.
ANDYGEN
That's the third fake.
ROOK
Fourth. You trusted the first one.
Maya found
MAYA
You took your time.
ANDYGEN
I came to rescue you.
MAYA
Then you're about fourteen minutes late.
ROOK
Fourteen minutes, twenty-two seconds.
MAYA
I like him.
Mission end
MAYA
The network isn't just hiding signals. It's creating new ones.
ROOK
Agreed. Deception appears intentional.
2-3

Acid Front / «Кислотный фронт»

Вводим Lena и адаптивность заражённого кода.

Entry / Lena intro
LENA
Those green bands are concentrated sulfuric aerosols. Avoid prolonged exposure.
ANDYGEN
You could have led with “it melts shields.”
LENA
I assumed that part was visually obvious.
Sample capture
LENA
I have the infected weather drone. Keep the channel isolated.
MAX
Sending you a sandbox. If it explodes, that's normal.
LENA
Define normal.
MAX
Recoverable.
Analysis
LENA
It changed while I was reading it.
ROOK
Clarify.
LENA
The code noticed the analysis.
MAX
That's not a virus.
2-4

Atmospheric Citadel / «Атмосферная цитадель»

Команда возвращает себе сенсоры и получает первый аргумент, что SOLAR может быть переносчиком, а не автором команд.

Entry
MAYA
Three jammer nodes around the station. Kill them and I get my eyes back.
ANDYGEN
You say that like I took them.
MAYA
You were late. Same category.
Node 1
ROOK
Sensor latency reduced by thirty-one percent.
Node 2
ANDYGEN
Tell me the third one turns the weather off.
MAYA
Sure. Let's pretend it does.
Node 3 / data
ROOK
Command traffic recovered. Source: SOLAR network. Structure: nonstandard.
LENA
SOLAR is carrying the commands. That doesn't mean SOLAR wrote them.
VALE
That's a dangerous distinction.
LENA
It's still a distinction.
2-5

Voice in the Storm / «Голос в буре»

Veil Hunter и первое прямое обращение SOLAR к Andygen.

Boss approach
ROOK
Large contact entering the storm cell. Sensor lock unstable.
MAYA
Ignore the radar. Watch the engine flare.
Phase 1
ROOK P2
Multiple echoes. One physical hull.
Phase 2
LENA
Lightning is exposing its hull. Use the flashes.
MAYA
There — right side. Now.
Phase 3
ROOK P2
Core overload after each strike. Window: one point eight seconds.
ANDYGEN
Plenty.
MAYA
I've heard that one.
Boss down / unknown channel
ANDYGEN
Storm's clearing.
SOLAR
PILOT ANDYGEN. TURN BACK.
ANDYGEN
ROOK?
ROOK
That was not me.
MAYA
Tell me you recorded that.
ROOK
Every sample.
Chapter end
VALE
Earth Orbital Defense just entered full lockdown.
ANDYGEN
Because of us?
VALE
I don't know. You're coming home.

Chapter fail / retry lines

Не делать их длинными: игрок должен хотеть нажать Retry, а не слушать сцену повторно.

Generic failure pool
ROOK
Hull integrity critical. Mission lost.
ROOK
Combat capability exhausted. Recommend immediate retry.
MAX
I can rebuild the ship. Try not to make it a habit.
MAYA
Next time, try surviving the dramatic entrance.
Maya failure line доступна только после 2-2. Max — после первого возвращения в ангар. Не повторять одну и ту же реплику два раза подряд.
