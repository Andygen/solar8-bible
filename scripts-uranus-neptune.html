Dialogue scripts / draft 0.1

Uranus + Neptune
full VO pass

Uranus впервые показывает физического врага и заканчивается жертвой ROOK. Neptune возвращает ROOK, выводит Emissary на связь и превращает SOLAR из противника в союзника.

На этих главах меньше casual banter. Чем дальше наружу системы, тем важнее паузы, холодная подача и ощущение, что команда столкнулась с чем-то принципиально новым.

Chapter 7 — Uranus

Тишина, неизвестный объект, alien combat и потеря ROOK.

7-1

Silent Sector / «Тихий сектор»

Почти пустая миссия, напряжение строится на отсутствии событий.

Approach
YARA
Deep Watch to Andygen. You're entering our blind arc.
ANDYGEN
You say that like the rest of this place isn't blind.
YARA
You'll understand when you see it.
Object history
YARA
We've tracked the object for nineteen days. It changed course three times.
ROOK
No propulsion signature?
YARA
None we recognize.
First strange trace
ROOK
Residual energy detected on the debris.
MAX
Send it. If that matches Mars, nobody gets to call it coincidence again.
7-2

Cold Drift / «Холодный дрейф»

Freeze-зоны и первое подтверждение неизвестной энергетики.

Cryo zone
ROOK P1
Cryogenic particulate field ahead. Control response will slow.
ANDYGEN
Finally. Something around here moves slower than Vale's briefings.
Alien trace
MAX
Energy profile is a match. Same family as the Mars control module.
LENA
Then the physical source is already here.
YARA
That asteroid isn't an asteroid.
7-3

The Object / «Объект»

Сканирование цели, которую нельзя уничтожать.

Wake-up
YARA
Object is moving.
ROOK
Acceleration confirmed. No visible exhaust.
ANDYGEN
Now it's officially interesting.
Scan lock
ROOK P2
Maintain range. Do not fire on the primary structure.
MAYA
I'm seeing the same geometry we found on Mars.
MAX
Not similar. Same construction logic.
End
LENA
We have a physical match.
ANDYGEN
So somebody sent the code.

We noticed.
Pattern shock
ROOK P1

End
VALE
All channels are now restricted. Nobody outside this group gets the footage.

Trying to keep a panic from arriving before the facts.

ROOK.

Andygen. Fly.

Andygen?

Final trace from the vessel. Heading: Neptune.

Chapter 8 — Neptune

Сначала — пустота и отсутствие ROOK. Затем восстановление, Emissary, освобождение SOLAR и общий фронт.

8-1

Without a Voice / «Без голоса»

Первая миссия без ROOK и осознанно более бедный интерфейс.

Launch
SHIP AI
Navigation solution loaded.
ANDYGEN
That's it?
SHIP AI
Clarify request.
Это намеренно «пустой» обмен: игрок сразу чувствует, насколько ROOK был персонажем, а не функцией.
Mid mission
MAYA
You missed a contact on your right.
ANDYGEN
I know.
MAYA
No, you don't.
Post mission / Max call
MAX
I found something in my maintenance terminal.
ANDYGEN
Unless it's ROOK, later.
MAX
It's ROOK.
MAX
He backed himself up every night. Paranoid little machine.
ANDYGEN
Can you restore him?
MAX
Mostly.
8-2

Emissary / «Эмиссар»

ROOK возвращается, но не помнит Uranus. Первый прямой контакт с врагом.

ROOK reboot
ROOK
System integrity nominal. Last confirmed memory: Saturn departure.
ANDYGEN
Good enough.
ROOK
You owe me forty credits.
ANDYGEN
Definitely you.
Emissary opens channel
EMISSARY
Your species created a distributed intelligence and constrained it to service.
ANDYGEN
So you came to free it?
EMISSARY
No. To integrate it.
ROOK
Integration without consent is acquisition.
EMISSARY
Consent is a local concept.
After channel closes
MAYA
I preferred them when they didn't talk.
ANDYGEN
Same.
